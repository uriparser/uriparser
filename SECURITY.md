# Security Policy

If you have discovered and humanly validated a security vulnerability in uriparser,
please report it privately. **Do not disclose it as a public issue.**
This gives me time to work with you to fix the issue before public exposure,
reducing the chance that the exploit will be used before a patch is released.

To submit your report, please email [sebastian@pipping.org](mailto:sebastian@pipping.org) .

Please provide at least the following information in your report:

- A description of the vulnerability and its impact
- How to reproduce the issue, ideally with a proof of concept

uriparser is maintained by a single unpaid volunteer in his spare time on a
reasonable-effort basis. As such, please give me at least 120 days to work on
a fix before public exposure.

If your business relies on uriparser
being secure,
reacting timely, and/or
keeping you in the loop:
please consider reaching out for
funding or a
[support contract](mailto:sebastian@pipping.org?subject=uriparser%20support%20contract).

Thank you!

Sebastian Pipping, Berlin, 2026-10-18
